## Stack

## Users

Primary: owners and investors of beverage, water and edible-oil plants — mostly in the Middle East, North Africa and Latin America (per testimonials: Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Libya, Bolivia, etc.) — who are planning a new bottling plant or expanding/upgrading a line. Often first-time buyers dealing with an overseas supplier, so trust in after-sales support is the deciding factor. Job: understand that SCIL can supply the whole line, believe they will be supported after installation, and send an enquiry (form, WhatsApp, WeChat, phone).

Secondary: plant engineers checking machine models, outputs (BPH) and container ranges; start-ups looking for small, priced machines (SOLO).

## Product Purpose

SCIL Machinery (Suzhou, China; "Taiwanese standard") designs and manufactures complete beverage packaging lines: preform injection, PET stretch blow moulding, filling (water, CSD, juice, hot fill, oil, 5-gallon, aseptic, anti-corrosive liquids), labeling, packaging (shrink, wrap-around case packing) and palletizing/smart warehouse, plus water treatment and conveyors. Success = qualified line enquiries from plant owners.

## Positioning

One supplier for the whole line, from preform to pallet, with installation, commissioning, line ramp-up, efficiency audits, breakdown support, spare parts and operator training. Clients repeatedly cite after-sales service and being "treated as a partner" — including working in difficult markets (post-war Libya) — as the reason they chose and re-bought from SCIL. SOLO offers fixed-price entry machines for start-ups, removing the intermediary.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate from offices and on phones, often via WhatsApp/WeChat; catalogue PDF (/SCIL-Catalogue.pdf) is a common hand-off. Throughput is spoken in BPH (bottles per hour); container sizes in litres / gallons. Existing site is trilingual (English, Arabic, Chinese); this pass is English, with Arabic RTL as a future need.

## Product Principles

1. Show the whole line, not a pile of machines — the preform-to-pallet sequence is the product.
2. After-sales trust is the proof; let clients say it with their real numbers.
3. Speak the buyer's units (BPH, litres, gallons) and let them reach a human fast (WhatsApp/phone).
4. Honest and specific over generic industrial superlatives.
