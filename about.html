Built in Suzhou to Taiwanese standards

SCIL Machinery is a large independent manufacturer of beverage packaging machinery, supplying complete lines since 2003.

23+ years. 840+ clients. 5,200 machines. 230B+ containers.

Our commitment is to clients looking for the best packaging solution, built on our own research, development and production. We always strive to provide better quality and better service.

SCIL offers complete beverage filling and packaging solutions covering every step: blow moulding, filling, labelling and packaging, for water, soft drinks, juices and homecare products. Our line services include installation and commissioning, line ramp-up, efficiency audits and breakdown support.

Vision

To become the benchmark for beverage packaging solutions.

Mission

To give our customers real value in every dealing with us: understand their requirements precisely, and supply economically manufactured machines of superior quality that meet or exceed their expectations.

Responsibility

SCIL promotes, at every level and in all its business, sustainability, environmental care, fair labour standards and the safety and health of workers, in full compliance with applicable law and guided by integrity and honesty.
